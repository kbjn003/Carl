Privacy Policy for CARL

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Who uses CARL

CARL is a personal assistant used only by me, its creator. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data CARL accesses

CARL accesses my own Google account data: Gmail messages and Google Calendar events.

How the data is used

The data is used only to create summaries and reminders for me, such as flagging emails that need my attention and telling me what is coming up on my calendar.

Storage and sharing

The data is not sold or shared with anyone. It may be processed by the AI and messaging services I use to run the assistant, only for the purpose described above.

Removing access

CARL's access can be removed at any time from the Google Account settings page, under third-party apps and services.
